Privacy Policy for Image Compressor

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Image Compressor ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and privacy.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

All image compression, resizing, format conversion, and comparisons are performed entirely on your device.

  • The app does not upload your photos or files to any external server or cloud service.
  • The app does not require or use the INTERNET permission (android.permission.INTERNET).
  • No network requests are made by the application at any time.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No Image Data: Your photos never leave your device.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: The app does not include any analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics), crash reporters, or tracking libraries.
  • No Advertising: The app contains no advertisements and integrates no ad SDKs.

3. Device Permissions

The app only requests the minimum necessary permissions to function:

  • Photo Picker (No Storage Permission): On modern Android versions, the app uses the system Photo Picker, which allows you to pick specific images without granting access to your entire storage library.
  • Storage Access (Android 9 / API 28 only): On Android 9, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is requested solely to save compressed images to your device's public Pictures directory. On Android 10 and above, standard scoped storage (MediaStore and Storage Access Framework) is used without requiring broad storage permissions.

4. Local Storage and Data Deletion

  • Temporary Cache: Temporary preview files are stored in the app's private internal cache (cache/outputs). These files are automatically cleaned up when starting a new session or clearing app cache.
  • History: The app maintains a local database of your saved compression history (file names, sizes, and timestamps) solely for your reference. This history is stored strictly on your device and can be cleared at any time via the History or Settings screen.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local app data, cache, and history.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
