Privacy Policy for Image Compressor

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Image Compressor ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and privacy.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

All image compression, resizing, format conversion, and comparisons are performed entirely on your device.

  • The app does not upload your photos, files, file names or image contents to any server or cloud service.
  • The INTERNET permission is used only to send the optional, anonymous diagnostics described below.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files. To find bugs and improve the app, it uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, which collect:

  • Crash reports and diagnostics: stack traces of crashes and unexpected errors, app version, device model, Android version and similar technical information.
  • App usage statistics: which screens are opened, and anonymous counts such as how many images were compressed, the chosen mode, output format and resize type, how many succeeded or failed, and the overall percentage of space saved. Never file names, image sizes or contents.
  • App identifiers: a random, app-specific installation ID generated by Firebase, not linked to your identity.
  • No advertising ID: collection of the Android advertising ID and ad personalisation are disabled.
  • No advertising: the app contains no advertisements and no ad SDKs.

Your choice: diagnostics are on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Share diagnostics. When turned off, no analytics events or crash reports are sent.

This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS), used only to improve the app, and is not sold or shared for advertising.

3. Device Permissions

The app only requests the minimum necessary permissions to function:

  • Photo Picker (No Storage Permission): On modern Android versions, the app uses the system Photo Picker, which allows you to pick specific images without granting access to your entire storage library.
  • Storage Access (Android 9 / API 28 only): On Android 9, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is requested solely to save compressed images to your device's public Pictures directory. On Android 10 and above, standard scoped storage (MediaStore and Storage Access Framework) is used without requiring broad storage permissions.

4. Local Storage and Data Deletion

  • Temporary Cache: Temporary preview files are stored in the app's private internal cache (cache/outputs). These files are automatically cleaned up when starting a new session or clearing app cache.
  • History: The app maintains a local database of your saved compression history (file names, sizes, and timestamps) solely for your reference. This history is stored strictly on your device and can be cleared at any time via the History or Settings screen.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local app data, cache, and history.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data sent is the optional, anonymous diagnostics described above, which never include images or personal details.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
