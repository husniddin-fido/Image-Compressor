Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 • Effective date: October 16, 2026

Image Compressor respects your privacy. Your images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. The only data that leaves your device is optional, anonymous diagnostics (crash reports and usage statistics), which you can turn off at any time in the app.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

All image processing operations — including image compression, resizing, format conversion (JPEG, PNG, WebP), and before/after comparisons — are performed exclusively on your physical device using local hardware resources.

  • No photos, files, file names or image contents are ever uploaded to any server or cloud service.
  • The app uses the android.permission.INTERNET permission only to send the optional, anonymous diagnostics described below.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files. To find bugs and improve the app, it uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, which collect:

  • Crash reports and diagnostics: stack traces of crashes and unexpected errors, app version, device model, Android version, and similar technical information.
  • App usage statistics: which screens are opened, and anonymous counts such as how many images were compressed, the chosen mode, output format and resize type, how many succeeded or failed, and the overall percentage of space saved. Never file names, image sizes or contents.
  • App identifiers: a random, app-specific installation ID generated by Firebase. It is not linked to your identity.
  • No advertising ID: collection of the Android advertising ID and ad personalisation are disabled.
  • No advertisements: the app does not show ads or include ad SDKs.

Your choice: diagnostics are on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Share diagnostics. When turned off, no analytics events or crash reports are sent.

This data is processed by Google according to the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS), used only to improve the app, and is not sold or shared for advertising.

3. Permissions & System Access

The app only utilizes the minimum operating system facilities required for core utility features:

  • Photo Picker (Android 10+): The app uses the official Android Photo Picker. This ensures that you only share the exact photos you choose; the app never has access to your full media library.
  • Storage Permission (Android 9 / API 28 only): On Android 9, the legacy WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission (scoped to maxSdkVersion="28") is used solely to save your compressed results to the public Pictures/Image Compressor folder. Modern scoped storage is utilized on API 29 and higher without requiring broad storage permissions.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

  • Temporary Cache: Temporary working files are written to the app's sandboxed private cache directory (cache/outputs). These files are automatically deleted upon starting a new session or finishing an existing one.
  • Saved History: Compression records (file names, previous and new byte sizes, dimensions, and timestamps) are kept solely on your device in a local SQLite database for your convenience. You can clear this history anytime in the app settings.
  • App Removal: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all associated local cache, preferences, and history from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Image Compressor does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data sent is the optional, anonymous diagnostics described above, which never include images or personal details.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published at this permanent URL with an updated effective date.

Maxfiylik Siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 16-oktabr, 2026-yil • Kuchga kirish sanasi: 16-oktabr, 2026-yil

Image Compressor maxfiyligingizni hurmat qiladi. Rasmlaringiz to'liq qurilmangizning o'zida qayta ishlanadi va hech qachon yuklanmaydi. Qurilmadan faqat ixtiyoriy, anonim diagnostika ma'lumotlari (nosozlik hisobotlari va foydalanish statistikasi) yuboriladi; ularni ilovada istalgan vaqtda o'chirib qo'yishingiz mumkin.

1. 100% Qurilmada Qayta Ishlash

Barcha rasm siqish, o'lchamini o'zgartirish, formatlarni konvertatsiya qilish (JPEG, PNG, WebP) va taqqoslash operatsiyalari faqat sizning qurilmangizning o'zida amalga oshiriladi.

  • Rasmlar, fayllar, fayl nomlari va rasm mazmuni hech qachon serverlarga yoki bulutli xizmatlarga yuklanmaydi.
  • android.permission.INTERNET ruxsati faqat quyida tavsiflangan ixtiyoriy, anonim diagnostikani yuborish uchun ishlatiladi.

2. Biz To'playdigan Ma'lumotlar

Ismingiz, elektron pochtangiz, telefon raqamingiz, kontaktlaringiz, aniq joylashuvingiz, rasmlaringiz yoki fayllaringiz to'planmaydi. Xatolarni topish va ilovani yaxshilash uchun Google Firebase Crashlytics va Google Analytics for Firebase ishlatiladi. Ular quyidagilarni to'playdi:

  • Nosozlik hisobotlari va diagnostika: ilova qulashi va kutilmagan xatolarning texnik izlari (stack trace), ilova versiyasi, qurilma modeli, Android versiyasi va shunga o'xshash texnik ma'lumotlar.
  • Foydalanish statistikasi: qaysi ekranlar ochilgani hamda anonim sonlar: nechta rasm siqilgani, tanlangan rejim, chiqish formati va o'lcham o'zgartirish turi, nechtasi muvaffaqiyatli yoki muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'lgani, umumiy tejalgan joy foizi. Fayl nomlari, rasm hajmlari yoki mazmuni hech qachon yuborilmaydi.
  • Ilova identifikatori: Firebase yaratadigan tasodifiy, faqat shu ilovaga tegishli o'rnatish ID'si. U shaxsingiz bilan bog'lanmaydi.
  • Reklama ID'si yo'q: Android reklama identifikatorini yig'ish va reklamani shaxsiylashtirish o'chirilgan.
  • Reklamasiz: ilovada reklama va reklama SDK'lari yo'q.

Sizning tanlovingiz: diagnostika sukut bo'yicha yoqilgan va uni istalgan vaqtda Settings → Share diagnostics orqali o'chirib qo'yish mumkin. O'chirilganda hech qanday statistika yoki nosozlik hisoboti yuborilmaydi.

Bu ma'lumotlar Google tomonidan Firebase maxfiylik ma'lumotlari va Google Maxfiylik Siyosati asosida qayta ishlanadi. Ular shifrlangan holda (HTTPS) yuboriladi, faqat ilovani yaxshilash uchun ishlatiladi, sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ulashilmaydi.

3. Tizim Ruxsatlari

Ilova faqat uning to'g'ri ishlashi uchun zarur bo'lgan minimal tizim imkoniyatlaridan foydalanadi:

  • Photo Picker (Android 10+): Ilova rasmiy Android Photo Picker tizimidan foydalanadi. Bu orqali siz faqat o'zingiz tanlagan rasmlarnigina taqdim etasiz, butun galereyaga ruxsat berish shart emas.
  • Xotiraga yozish (faqat Android 9 / API 28): Faqat Android 9 da siqilgan rasmni Pictures/Image Compressor papkasiga saqlash uchun ruxsat so'raladi. Android 10 va undan yuqori versiyalarda umumiy ruxsatlarsiz zamonaviy Scoped Storage ishlatiladi.

4. Ma'lumotlarni Saqlash va O'chirish

  • Vaqtinchalik kesh: Qayta ishlash jarayonidagi vaqtinchalik fayllar ilovaning shaxsiy kesh papkasida (cache/outputs) saqlanadi va yangi sessiya boshlanganda avtomatik o'chiriladi.
  • Saqlash tarixi: Saqlangan rasmlar tarixi (fayl nomi, hajmi, sanasi) faqat sizga qulaylik uchun qurilma xotirasida (Room DB) saqlanadi. Uni istalgan vaqtda sozlamalardan o'chirib tashlashingiz mumkin.
  • Ilovani o'chirish: Ilovani telefondan o'chirib tashlaganingizda, unga tegishli barcha kesh va tarixlar birgalikda to'liq o'chib ketadi.
